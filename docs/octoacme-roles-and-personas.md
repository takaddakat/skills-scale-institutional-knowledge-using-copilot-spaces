# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## Business Analysts

### Role Summary
Business Analysts translate business objectives into clear requirements, process improvements, and measurable outcomes. They help teams align operational needs with delivery priorities and ensure that changes are grounded in user and stakeholder needs.

### Responsibilities
- Gather and document business requirements, workflows, and constraints
- Clarify scope, dependencies, and acceptance criteria with stakeholders and developers
- Identify process gaps, inefficiencies, and opportunities for improvement
- Support prioritization decisions by connecting initiatives to business value
- Validate that implemented solutions meet stakeholder expectations

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Works closely with Product Managers to refine problems, priorities, and success measures
- Partners with Project Managers to align requirements and delivery planning with milestones
- Supports Developers and Test Leads by translating stakeholder needs into actionable acceptance criteria and test scenarios
- Coordinates with business stakeholders and subject matter experts to confirm assumptions and resolve ambiguity

### Goals
- Improve clarity around requirements and outcomes
- Reduce rework caused by unclear scope or assumptions
- Strengthen alignment between business needs and delivery execution

### Typical Communication
- Requirements workshops and interviews
- Process mapping and backlog refinement discussions
- Stakeholder sign-offs, issue clarification, and acceptance reviews

---

## Change and Adoption Leads

### Role Summary
Change and Adoption Leads help teams prepare for and adopt new processes, tools, or business changes. They reduce disruption by building readiness plans, communication strategies, and training support that align with project milestones.

### Responsibilities
- Develop change impact assessments and stakeholder readiness plans
- Coordinate communications, training, and enablement activities
- Identify adoption barriers and mitigation strategies across teams and departments
- Partner with leadership to reinforce new behaviors and process changes
- Measure readiness and adoption progress during and after rollout

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Works with Project Managers to sequence communications and adoption activities with project milestones
- Coordinates with Product Managers and Business Analysts to ensure user-facing changes match stakeholder needs and expected outcomes
- Supports Developers and Quality Leads by helping validate usability, training readiness, and release communication needs
- Collaborates with Sponsors and leadership to reinforce adoption goals and executive sponsorship

### Goals
- Increase stakeholder readiness and adoption of changes
- Reduce resistance and confusion during transitions
- Improve the likelihood of sustained business value after implementation

### Typical Communication
- Change impact assessments and adoption plans
- Training sessions, FAQs, and stakeholder briefings
- Readiness checkpoints and post-launch feedback reviews

---

## Quality and Test Leads

### Role Summary
Quality and Test Leads define the testing strategy, quality gates, and validation approach for project work. They ensure that deliverables meet agreed standards and that quality is addressed early in the project lifecycle.

### Responsibilities
- Define quality standards, test strategy, and validation plans
- Coordinate test case development, execution, and defect triage
- Partner with development teams to identify risks, defects, and release readiness criteria
- Support acceptance testing and stakeholder validation activities
- Track quality trends and recommend improvements to reduce defects and rework

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Works with Developers to align test coverage, defect prioritization, and release readiness criteria
- Supports Product Managers and Business Analysts by validating that requirements are measurable and testable
- Coordinates with Project Managers to integrate quality checkpoints into delivery plans and milestones
- Advises Security and Privacy Leads on validation for controls, compliance, and operational risk

### Goals
- Deliver high-quality outcomes with fewer defects and escalations
- Improve confidence in release decisions and business readiness
- Build quality into delivery rather than relying on late-stage validation

### Typical Communication
- Test plans, quality dashboards, and defect reports
- Release readiness reviews and acceptance criteria discussions
- Cross-functional quality check-ins and root cause analysis sessions

---

## Security and Privacy Leads

### Role Summary
Security and Privacy Leads provide governance, controls, and risk guidance to ensure that project work complies with necessary standards and protects sensitive information. They help teams identify security and privacy implications before they become blockers.

### Responsibilities
- Evaluate project risks related to security, privacy, compliance, and data handling
- Define controls, review requirements, and advise on mitigations
- Partner with technical teams to assess architecture, access, and data protection decisions
- Review project changes for compliance and operational risk impacts
- Support escalation pathways for high-risk findings and remediation planning

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Works with Project Managers to incorporate security and privacy risk reviews into project plans and approvals
- Collaborates with Developers and Technical Leads to assess architectural vulnerabilities and data handling decisions
- Supports Product Managers and Business Analysts in understanding compliance constraints and user/privacy implications
- Coordinates with Quality Leads to validate control effectiveness and release readiness requirements

### Goals
- Reduce security and privacy risks before they impact delivery
- Maintain compliance with required standards and operating policies
- Improve trust and confidence in project outcomes and data handling

### Typical Communication
- Risk assessments, security reviews, and remediation discussions
- Compliance checkpoints and architecture review meetings
- Escalation briefs for high-impact issues and governance decisions

---

## Portfolio and PMO Analysts

### Role Summary
Portfolio and PMO Analysts support governance, prioritization, and project performance management across multiple initiatives. They help ensure that work is aligned to strategy, resource constraints, and organizational reporting needs.

### Responsibilities
- Maintain portfolio views of initiatives, status, risks, dependencies, and priority trade-offs
- Support governance reporting, executive updates, and decision-making needs
- Help compare projects against strategic objectives, ROI, and resource capacity
- Facilitate reporting and cross-project alignment on shared dependencies
- Support project standardization and documentation practices across teams

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Works with Project Managers to ensure portfolio health, dependencies, and reporting are aligned with delivery execution
- Partners with Product Managers to assess strategic priority and value-based trade-offs
- Coordinates with Sponsors and leadership to surface cross-project risks, benefits, and resourcing challenges
- Supports Business Analysts and Change Leads by helping contextualize initiative impact and readiness across programs

### Goals
- Improve decision-making across the portfolio
- Increase transparency into project health and strategic alignment
- Reduce duplication, conflicts, and missed dependencies across initiatives

### Typical Communication
- Portfolio reviews and executive dashboards
- Dependency tracking and project health reporting
- Governance updates and prioritization discussions

## How these personas are used in the exercise
- Use these persona definitions to frame scenarios and sample interactions in the Skills Exercise.
- Each persona can be used as a persona prompt for Copilot Spaces to shape role-specific guidance.
- When a project needs broader accountability or governance support, these additional roles can be added to clarify responsibilities, communication patterns, and handoffs across the project lifecycle.
